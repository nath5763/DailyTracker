# DailyTracker.cc — Marketing & Growth Idea Tracker

**Goal:** Grow DailyTracker into a go-to leaderboard for friend groups playing daily games, starting with Discord communities.

**90-day target (hypothesis):** 50 retained, actively scoring groups.  
**Primary success metric:** Groups with multiple players submitting scores consistently, not just signups.

> Status key: ⬜ Not started · 🟡 In progress · ✅ Done · ⏸️ Paused. Replace status icons as work progresses.
